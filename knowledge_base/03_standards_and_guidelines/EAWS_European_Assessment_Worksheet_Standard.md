# European Assessment Worksheet (EAWS) Industrial Standard
**Authors:** K. Schaub, G. Caragnano, B. Britzke, R. Bruder (2012)  
**Industry Adoption:** BMW Group, Volkswagen AG, Stellantis, Audi, Porsche  
**Scope:** Cycle-based holistic physical workload evaluation

## 1. Structure of EAWS Sections
1. **Section 0:** General task organizational parameters (cycle time, duration, shift models).
2. **Section 1:** Static Postures (standing, sitting, kneeling, bending, trunk inclination, reach).
3. **Section 2:** Action Forces (whole-body pushing, pulling, foot pedal operation).
4. **Section 3:** Manual Material Handling (lifting, lowering, carrying with load weight matrix).
5. **Section 4:** Upper Limbs / Repetitive Movements (shoulder abduction, forearm rotation, wrist flexion).

## 2. Scoring System and Traffic Light Tiers
Total Cycle Score:
$$\text{Total EAWS Points} = P_{\text{posture}} + P_{\text{force}} + P_{\text{handling}} + P_{\text{upper\_limbs}}$$

Traffic Light Evaluation:
- **Green (0 to 25 points):** Low risk. Workstation compliant with automotive labor standards.
- **Yellow (26 to 50 points):** Possible risk. Workstation requires redesign or rotation within 6 months.
- **Red (> 50 points):** High risk. Immediate engineering intervention mandatory.
