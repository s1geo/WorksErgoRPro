# ISO 11228-1/2/3 and ISO 11226 International Standards Compendium
**Standardization Body:** International Organization for Standardization (ISO / TC 159 / SC 3)

## 1. ISO 11228-1: Manual Lifting, Lowering and Carrying
- Reference Mass: $m_{\text{ref}} = 25\text{ kg}$ (males, working population), $m_{\text{ref}} = 15\text{ kg}$ (females / general).
- Recommended Weight Limit:
  $$RWL = m_{\text{ref}} \cdot H_M \cdot V_M \cdot D_M \cdot A_M \cdot F_M \cdot C_M$$
- Lifting Index: $LI = \text{Load} / RWL$. Target $LI \le 1.0$.

## 2. ISO 11228-2: Pushing and Pulling
- Initial Force Limit ($F_{\text{initial}}$) vs Sustained Force Limit ($F_{\text{sustained}}$).
- Snook-Ciriello psychophysical grounding.

## 3. ISO 11228-3: Low Loads at High Frequency (Upper Limbs)
- OCRA method integration. Daily exposure indices.

## 4. ISO 11226: Evaluation of Static Working Postures
- Non-acceptable trunk flexion without support: $> 60^\circ$.
- Non-acceptable head extension: $> 0^\circ$.
- Non-acceptable arm elevation without support: $> 60^\circ$.
