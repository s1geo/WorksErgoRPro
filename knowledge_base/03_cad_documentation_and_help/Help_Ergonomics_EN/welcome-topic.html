Description

The R-Pro add-on "Ergonomics" is designed for the correct organization of the technological process. Works in tandem with the add-on "MoCap".

Implemented assessment methods:

- RULA (Rapid Upper Limb Assessment)

- REBA (Rapid Entire Body Assessment)

- ISO 11226 - Ergonomics — Evaluation of static working postures

Ergonomics is a comprehensive science that studies human interaction in working conditions to improve the efficiency, safety and comfort of these conditions.

The RULA method mainly evaluates the upper limbs (shoulders, elbows, wrists), as well as the neck and trunk. The RULA method mainly evaluates posture. It is applied to tasks during which the operator mainly uses the upper limbs, with or without movement. Postures are mainly studied taking into account adjustments related to the applied force and the repetition of the gesture. This method is not recommended for the assessment of manual handling tasks.

The REBA method evaluates the various required body postures and their difficulty. It assigns a score to each of the following body parts: wrists, forearms, elbows, shoulders, neck, trunk, back, legs and knees. The method tables are then used to compile the variable risk factors, generating a single score representing the level of MSD risk. The REBA method uses a scoring system to evaluate postural assessments covering static, dynamic, changing or unstable postures. The REBA score provides a postural risk score, allowing the severity of a posture to be quantified.

ISO 11226 standard allows to determine recommended boundary values ​​of parameters of static working positions, limit angles between joints of the body and temporary values ​​of parameters of maintaining static (fixed) working positions without or with minimal external efforts. Allows to evaluate acceptability of static working positions and to identify risks of damage to health of the worker.
