Selecting Materials

You can select a material being used by more than one object in the 3D world. This is similar to picking, but you are selecting the material of multiple objects.

  1. On the Modeling tab, in the Geometry group, click the Tools arrow, and then under Material, click Assign.
  2. In the Assign material task pane, expand Materials, and then right-click a material, and then click Select All Using. Objects with that material will be outlined in the 3D world, but understand you are selecting a material not an object.
