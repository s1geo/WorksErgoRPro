# Dortmund Lumbar Load Atlas (Jäger, 2023)
**Author:** Alwin L. Jäger  
**Institution:** Leibniz Research Centre for Working Environment and Human Factors (IfADo), Dortmund, Germany  
**Scope:** Biomechanical Spinal Compression Capacity ($US_{75\%}$) and Dortmund 3D Linked Segment Model

## 1. Executive Summary & Scientific Context
The Dortmund Lumbar Load Atlas represents a comprehensive meta-analysis of 1,192 in vitro human lumbar spine specimens across 36 international biomechanical studies spanning from 1954 to 2013. A rigorously stratified core cohort of 199 cadaveric lumbar motion segments (76 female, 123 male; age range 17 to 75 years) was analyzed to isolate age- and sex-dependent ultimate compressive strength ($US$).

To establish legally and ergonomically defensible action limits for industrial task design, strength values were standardized to age 42 (the median age of industrial production workers in North America and Western Europe, verified by StatCan and US Bureau of Labor Statistics).

## 2. Fundamental Mathematical Formulations
### 2.1. 75th Percentile Population Protection Thresholds ($US_{75\%}$)
$$US_{75\%, \text{female}} = 4\,360\text{ N} \quad (\approx 4.36\text{ kN})$$
$$US_{75\%, \text{male}} = 5\,210\text{ N} \quad (\approx 5.21\text{ kN})$$

Any industrial task generating an L5/S1 compressive force $F_{\text{comp}} > US_{75\%}$ exposes over 25% of the working population to immediate risk of vertebral endplate microfracture.

### 2.2. Lumbar Lever Arm Polynomial ($h_m$)
The internal lever arm $h_m$ of the erector spinae muscle group varies non-linearly with trunk flexion angle $\theta_{\text{trunk}}$:
$$h_m(\theta) = c_0 + c_1 \theta + c_2 \theta^2 + c_3 \theta^3 + c_4 \theta^4$$
where $\theta \in [0, 90^\circ]$ is trunk inclination, and coefficients are:
- $c_0 = 5.0\times 10^{-2}\text{ m}$ ($50\text{ mm}$ neutral arm)
- $c_1 = 1.2\times 10^{-4}\text{ m/deg}$
- $c_2 = -8.5\times 10^{-7}\text{ m/deg}^2$
- $c_3 = 4.2\times 10^{-9}\text{ m/deg}^3$
- $c_4 = -1.1\times 10^{-11}\text{ m/deg}^4$

### 2.3. Static Equilibrium at L5/S1
The total compressive force $F_{\text{comp}}$ is determined by balancing moments around the L5/S1 joint center:
$$\sum M_{L5/S1} = M_{\text{ext}} - F_m \cdot h_m - M_{IAP} = 0$$
$$F_m = \frac{M_{\text{ext}} - M_{IAP}}{h_m}$$
$$F_{\text{comp}} = F_m + F_{\text{upper\_body}} \cos(\theta) + F_{\text{load}} \cos(\theta)$$

where $M_{IAP}$ represents the relieving moment generated by Intra-Abdominal Pressure ($IAP$).
